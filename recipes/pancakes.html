Good Old-Fashioned Pancakes

Description

Good Old-Fashioned Pancakes are simple homemade pancakes with a soft and fluffy texture.

They use basic pantry ingredients and are cooked on a hot pan until both sides are golden brown.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking powder
  • White sugar
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Combine the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Make a small well in the center of the dry ingredients.
  3. Add the milk, melted butter, and egg.
  4. Mix until the ingredients form a batter.
  5. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  6. Pour portions of batter onto the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear and the edges begin to set.
  8. Flip and cook the other side until golden brown.
