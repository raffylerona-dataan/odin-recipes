Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is a popular Filipino dish known for its savory, slightly tangy, and rich flavor.

The chicken is slowly cooked in a mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and seasonings until it becomes tender and absorbs the flavorful sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Bay leaves
  • Whole black peppercorns
  • Water
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken with soy sauce and garlic.
  2. Allow the chicken to absorb the marinade.
  3. Heat oil in a pot and lightly cook the chicken.
  4. Add the marinade, vinegar, bay leaves, peppercorns, and water.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then lower the heat.
  6. Simmer until the chicken is tender and the sauce has reduced.
  7. Serve the chicken adobo with rice.
